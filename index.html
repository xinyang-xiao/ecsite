02 Overview

Jewelry commerce,
made as a team.

EVRIS is a group project for a jewelry and natural-stone brand. The experience brings together product discovery, cart and checkout flows, members-only interactions, and store operations into one connected product.

07 Reflection

What I learned.

EVRIS strengthened my understanding of frontend development as part of an e-commerce journey: small interaction decisions shape how confidently people move from a product to a cart and checkout.

It also made team collaboration concrete. A credible commerce experience depends on design, frontend work, product data, operations and payment infrastructure coming together clearly.
